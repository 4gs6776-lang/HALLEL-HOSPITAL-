What We Offer

Comprehensive Medical Care

We offer a comprehensive range of medical services tailored to meet the needs of our community.

General Consultation

Expert medical consultations to diagnose and treat a wide variety of health conditions.

Antenatal Care (ANC)

Comprehensive check-ups and support for mothers throughout their pregnancy journey.

Maternity & Delivery

Safe, comfortable, and compassionate delivery services for mother and baby.

Obstetrics & Gynaecology

Specialized care for female reproductive health and pregnancy management.

Paediatrics

Dedicated healthcare for infants, children, and adolescents in a caring environment.

Medicine & Surgery

Advanced surgical procedures and internal medicine treatments by skilled professionals.

Laboratory Services

Accurate and timely diagnostic testing using modern laboratory equipment.

Ultrasound Scan

State-of-the-art imaging services for accurate diagnosis and fetal monitoring.

Eye Clinic

Comprehensive eye examinations and treatment for various vision and eye health issues.

Immunization

Essential vaccinations for children and adults to prevent infectious diseases.

Family Planning

Confidential counseling and contraceptive services for family health planning.

24/7 Emergency Services

Round-the-clock urgent medical care for critical and life-threatening situations.

HMO

Seamless healthcare access supported by various Health Maintenance Organizations.
